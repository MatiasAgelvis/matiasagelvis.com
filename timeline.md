personal projects (Starisk, 2021; CellSV; the CV generator) and 70+ self-directed courses.
Never describe it as a client portfolio, a studio, or consulting work. The honest framing —
*independent work, low volume, alongside self-directed study* — is enough to close the
timeline without a single invented client.

2022 interlude. The research assistantship is folded into **Formation**, one line, because

limit, and their matching rewards continuous history. Nothing is dropped from the timeline;
